## 1. You may not

**Unlawful or harmful content**

* upload, store or share content that is illegal, defamatory, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit involving minors, or that infringes someone's intellectual-property or privacy rights;
* store **special personal information / special-category data** (health, biometrics, religion, political opinion, criminal records, etc.), children's information, government identifiers or **payment-card data** in annotations, comments or captures. Pinpoint is not built or contracted for it.

**Capturing people's data**

* capture screenshots, console logs, network traces or session replay of pages or people without the legal right to do so, or without giving the required notice, or in a way that records credentials, tokens or other secrets;
* use the extension, widget or review links to covertly monitor individuals.

**Attacks and abuse**

* probe, scan or test the Service's security without our written permission (report issues to {{SECURITY_EMAIL}} instead — good-faith research that avoids data access and disruption will not be pursued);
* interfere with the Service: denial of service, excessive load, circumventing **rate limits**, quotas or plan limits, abusing public endpoints (guest feedback, widget, portals, boards) to spam or flood;
* attempt to access another organisation's data, other users' accounts, or non-public parts of the Service; share or sell access; reverse-engineer parts of the hosted service that are not open source where the law allows no such right;
* use the Service's outbound features (review-link proxy, integrations, webhooks) to attack, scan or reach internal networks or third-party systems, or to relay malware.

**Misuse of identities and messaging**

* impersonate a person or organisation, or send unsolicited bulk messages through invitations, notifications, portal magic links or webhooks;
* create accounts by automated means, or use disposable identities to evade suspension.

Account owners and admins are responsible for the people they invite, the guests who use their links, widgets and portals, and the API keys they create. Revoke keys and links that are no longer needed.
